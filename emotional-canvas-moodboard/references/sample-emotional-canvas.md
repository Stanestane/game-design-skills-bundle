# Sample Emotional Canvas

## Source concept

A solo Unity prototype about a character surviving accelerating falling blocks, collecting coins on settled stacks, and using arcade-style powerups.

## Target Feeling

**Cheerful pressure**

The player should feel constantly pressed by danger, but in a way that is lively, readable, and score-hungry rather than oppressive.

## Emotional Texture

- kinetic
- nimble
- bright but dangerous
- toy-like physicality
- improvisational survival
- greedy little bursts of risk-taking

## Emotional Field

### Adjacent

- arcade urgency
- playful danger
- bright chaos
- greedy score-chasing
- last-second escape

### Contrasting

- grim dread
- realistic disaster panic
- meditative block-stacking solemnity
- muddy visual overload
- punishing brutality

## Emotional Palette

### Visuals

- chunky falling geometry
- compact arenas
- readable negative space
- bright coin highlights
- saturated pickups against calmer surroundings
- strong silhouette separation

### Materials

- hard toy-block edges
- glossy pickups
- slightly industrial arena surfaces
- readable collision language

### Audio

- chirpy arcade stabs
- rising tempo pressure
- crisp pickup feedback
- playful panic rather than horror

### Environment

- compressed spaces
- visible overhead threat
- clean floor/readable stack silhouette
- escalating density over time

### Objects

- coins as temptation beacons
- powerups as rare candy-like intrusions
- stacked block masses as temporary shelter and trap

### Narrative tone

- mischievous
- unserious in premise, serious in moment-to-moment pressure
- prototype-friendly and immediately legible

## Anti-patterns

- photoreal rubble and disaster realism
- over-textured backgrounds
- washed-out brown-grey palettes
- solemn prestige-platformer melancholy
- effects spam that hides safe spaces and coin readability

## Design Heuristics

1. Readability over decoration.
2. Pressure over punishment.
3. Bright temptation over passive scoring.
4. Chunky geometry over messy debris.
5. Playful panic over dread.
6. Escalation over complexity.

## Moodboard brief

Seek images that communicate:

- overhead danger
- clean chunky geometry
- bright collectible contrast
- retro arcade pulse
- playful but real movement pressure

Prefer anchor references like:

- readable platformer movement games
- falling-block games with strong color language
- score-first arcade games with excellent HUD clarity
- geometric games with toy-like hazard readability
- explicit palette references with the right warm/cool balance, contrast rhythm, and collectible highlight behavior

Avoid references that imply:

- survival horror
- realistic destruction
- gloomy sludge palettes
- intricate simulation-heavy environments
- generic stock/game-adjacent filler that adds topic match without aesthetic value
